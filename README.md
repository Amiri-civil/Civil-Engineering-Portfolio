## 📚 Research

My research activities focus on the relationship between Civil Engineering education, water and environmental challenges, sustainability, and transportation systems.

### Bachelor’s Thesis
**Evaluation of Courses Related to Water, Environment, and Sustainable Development in Undergraduate Civil Engineering Programs at Selected Asian Universities**

A comparative study of water, environmental, and sustainable development-related courses in undergraduate Civil Engineering programs at selected Asian universities.

*(Full thesis in Persian; English translation available among the files.)*

### Conference Paper
**Evaluation of Water, Environmental, and Sustainable Development Courses in the Undergraduate Civil Engineering Curricula of Iranian Universities: A Comparison of the Old and New Curricula**

A comparative study of the coverage of water, environmental engineering, and sustainable development subjects in the old and revised Civil Engineering curricula of Iranian universities.

*(Presented and published at the 14th International Congress on Civil Engineering, Sharif University of Technology. Full paper in Persian; English translation available among the files. Available online through Civilica Website.)*
