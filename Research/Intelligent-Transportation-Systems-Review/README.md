# Journal Paper - Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS) in Iran

Investigating the role of intelligent transportation systems (ITS) with approach of road transportation and the implementation of the ITS system in Iran until the end of 1400

## 📌 Overview

This study investigates the role of Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS) in road transportation, focusing on their potential to improve traffic safety, operational efficiency, traffic management, and environmental performance. It also examines the implementation of ITS technologies across Iran up to the end of the Iranian year 1400 (2021–2022), using information reported by the Iran Road Maintenance and Transportation Organization.

## 🔬 Research Methodology

The study combines a review of ITS concepts and applications with an examination of reported ITS implementation in Iran. It discusses the functions and benefits of intelligent transportation technologies in road transportation and reviews data from the Iran Road Maintenance and Transportation Organization on the deployment of selected ITS equipment across Iranian provinces up to the end of 1400.

The analysis covers:

- ITS concepts, system architecture, and major applications
- The role of ITS in road safety, traffic management, and transportation efficiency
- Reported environmental and fuel-consumption benefits in selected international cities
- The distribution of ITS equipment across Iranian provinces
- The implementation of five categories of ITS equipment in Iran

## 📊 Key Findings

- **Improved traffic management:** ITS technologies can support traffic monitoring, road management, travel information services, and more efficient transportation operations.
- **Environmental benefits:** The article reports reductions in air pollution and fuel consumption associated with ITS applications in selected international cities.
- **ITS implementation in Iran:** The study examines five categories of equipment deployed on Iranian roads by the end of 1400: online traffic counters, weigh-in-motion systems, variable message signs, video surveillance cameras, and speed-control cameras.
- **Reported equipment deployment:** The article's tables report 2,594 online traffic counters, 83 weigh-in-motion systems, 221 variable message signs, 884 video surveillance cameras, and 2,008 speed-control cameras across the country's provinces.
- **Road safety and efficiency:** The study highlights the potential of ITS to improve road safety, monitor traffic conditions, reduce delays, and support more effective transportation management.

*Note: The reported equipment figures and implementation period reflect the data examined in the article; they should not be interpreted as current figures.*
