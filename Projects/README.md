# Civil Engineering Projects

This section presents a selection of academic projects completed during my undergraduate studies in Civil Engineering at Amirkabir University of Technology (Tehran Polytechnic). The projects cover hydrological modeling, water distribution system design, transportation engineering, and structural analysis and design.

Each project demonstrates the application of engineering principles, analytical methods, and specialized software to practical civil engineering problems. Project files, calculations, models, drawings, and available documentation are organized in their respective folders.


## Project Portfolio

## Engineering Areas

### Water and Hydrological Engineering
- Rainfall-runoff modeling for a catchment in Golestan Province, Iran, using HEC-HMS
- Modeling of seven subbasins and three main river reaches
- Sensitivity analysis of hydrological parameters and manual model calibration
- Design and hydraulic analysis of a water distribution network for the Yousefabad–Jahad Square area in Tehran
- Water demand estimation, network layout, pressure zoning, reservoir and pump evaluation, and water quality analysis

### Transportation Engineering
- Geometric design of an approximately 12 km collector-distributor road
- Manual and software-based design phases
- Horizontal and vertical alignment, longitudinal profiles, and road cross-sections
- Mass-haul diagram, road signage, and pavement marking plans

### Structural Engineering
- Structural modeling, analysis, and design of reinforced concrete and steel buildings
- Structural verification and design checks
- Beam and column grouping for reinforced concrete design
- Waffle slab and reinforced concrete staircase design
- Foundation design, including dimensions, thickness, and reinforcement detailing
  

## Project Work and Documentation

The projects were completed through a combination of individual and collaborative academic work:

- **Individual projects:** Rainfall-runoff modeling and both phases of the road geometric design
- **Collaborative projects:** Water distribution network design (three-member team) and concrete building design (two-member team)
- **Steel structure project:** Structural modeling, design, and verification work

Available project documentation may include engineering models, software files, design calculations, input data, simulation results, drawings, analysis outputs, and project reports. English versions or summaries are included where available.

The road project includes materials from both manual and software-based design phases. The hydrology project includes modeling and calibration documentation, while the water distribution project includes network modeling and hydraulic analysis materials.
