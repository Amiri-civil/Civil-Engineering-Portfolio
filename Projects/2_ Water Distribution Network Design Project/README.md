# Water Distribution Network Design Project

## Project Overview
This project involved the design and hydraulic analysis of a water distribution network for the Yousefabad–Jahad Square area in Tehran, Iran. The study considered an estimated population of 26,679 people and a 25-year design period, focusing on meeting water demand, maintaining adequate pressure, and supporting future network requirements.

## Project Execution
The project was carried out collaboratively by a three-member student team as part of the Water and Wastewater Engineering course at Amirkabir University of Technology during the first semester of the 2024–2025 academic year.

The work involved preparing the topographic data and network layout, estimating population and water demand, developing the distribution network model, and performing hydraulic simulations and water quality analysis using EPANET.

## Design Scope
- Preparation of the project area's topographic surface and elevation data
- Development of the network layout, including junctions, pipes, and interconnected loops
- Estimation of the design population and water demand based on land use and a 25-year design period
- Allocation of demand to network junctions and preparation of hydraulic input data
- Design and evaluation of reservoir storage capacity and pump requirements
- Pressure zoning and placement of pressure-reducing valves (PRVs)
- Selection of PVC pipes and determination of preliminary pipe diameters
- Hydraulic simulation and analysis of junction pressures, pipe flow rates, velocities, and friction losses
- Evaluation of network performance under hourly demand variations and relevant hydraulic design criteria
- Analysis of residual chlorine concentration and water quality within the distribution network
